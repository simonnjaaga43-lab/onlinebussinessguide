How Much Does It Cost to Build a Website in Kenya?

Before paying someone to build your website, understand what you are paying for. The price can vary significantly depending on what you need and how you choose to build it.

Imagine running a small business in Kenya and deciding that it is finally time to create a website.

You start asking around. One person quotes KSh 10,000. Another says KSh 40,000. Someone else suggests you need more than KSh 100,000.

Now you are left wondering: what is the actual cost of building a website?

The truth is that there is no single price. A simple website that introduces your business is very different from an online shop that manages products, orders and M-Pesa payments.

In this guide, we break down website development costs in Kenya, explain the expenses that often get overlooked, and help you decide which option makes sense for your business.

Important: The figures in this guide are indicative market estimates based on published Kenyan pricing information available in 2026. They are not fixed industry rates. Always request a current quotation and check what is included.

1. How much does a website cost in Kenya?

The cost depends mainly on the website's purpose, features, design requirements and who builds it.

As a general budgeting guide, consider these approximate ranges:

Website typeIndicative cost
Simple landing pageKSh 5,000–20,000
Small business websiteKSh 15,000–50,000
Larger company websiteKSh 35,000–120,000+
Small e-commerce websiteKSh 50,000–150,000+
Custom web applicationKSh 120,000 and above

These are broad planning estimates, not guaranteed quotations. Some providers charge less or considerably more depending on the work involved.

For example, a five-page website with a contact form and WhatsApp button requires less development than an online store with product management, delivery options and automated payment processing.

2. What are you actually paying for?

A website is not just a design on a screen. Several things work together to make it available and useful to visitors.

Domain name

A domain is your website's address, such as yourbusiness.co.ke.

For budgeting, a .co.ke domain may cost roughly KSh 1,000–2,500 per year, depending on the provider and renewal terms.

Web hosting

Hosting provides the infrastructure that stores your website files and makes them accessible online.

Basic shared hosting can start at a few thousand shillings per year. For example, TailoredHosting's published prices show introductory hosting prices alongside their renewal rates.

Design and development

This is the work involved in planning, designing and building the website. It may include page layouts, mobile-friendly design, navigation, contact forms and other features.

Content and images

Your website needs useful words and relevant images. If you hire someone to write the content, take photographs or create graphics, these may be additional expenses.

SSL and security

SSL helps protect the connection between your website and visitors. Many hosting providers include basic SSL certificates at no extra charge, so check what your package offers.

Maintenance

Websites may need updates, backups, security checks and technical assistance. Some owners manage simple sites themselves, while others pay a developer or maintenance provider.

3. Should you build the website yourself or hire someone?

This decision can make a major difference to your initial budget.

Option A: Build it yourself

If you have time and are willing to learn, you can create a website using website builders, WordPress or HTML and CSS.

For a simple informational website, you may be able to keep costs relatively low, especially if you use free tools and suitable hosting.

However, you are investing your own time. You will need to learn how to structure pages, organize content, make the website mobile-friendly and maintain it.

Best for: Beginners, small businesses with limited budgets and people interested in learning digital skills.

Option B: Hire a freelancer

A freelancer can build a website according to your requirements without the cost structure of a larger agency.

For a straightforward small business website, published Kenyan pricing examples commonly fall within the tens of thousands of shillings, although the final quotation depends on the scope.

Best for: Businesses that need a professional website but do not require a large development team.

Option C: Hire a web design agency

An agency may provide a team covering design, development, content, project management and support.

This can be useful for larger projects, but it may be unnecessary for a business that only needs a few informative pages and a way for customers to get in touch.

Best for: Businesses with more complex requirements, larger budgets or a need for several services under one provider.

4. Example budgets for three Kenyan businesses

Let's make the costs easier to understand through practical examples.

Example 1: A local salon

A salon wants customers to find its services, see photographs, check its location and contact the business through WhatsApp.

It may only need a simple website with a homepage, services page, gallery and contact page.

It does not necessarily need an online payment system or a complicated booking platform.

Example 2: A furniture business

A furniture maker wants to display completed tables, beds, cabinets and other projects.

The website could include a portfolio, service descriptions, customer enquiry form and contact details.

A simple website may be enough to help potential customers understand the quality of the work before making an enquiry.

Example 3: An online clothing shop

A clothing business wants customers to browse products, select items, place orders and pay online.

This requires more than displaying photographs. It may need product management, a shopping cart, checkout, payment integration and delivery arrangements.

That additional functionality explains why an online shop usually costs more than a basic business website.

5. Don't forget the recurring costs

One mistake business owners make is budgeting only for the initial website development.

Some website expenses return every year or month.

Some providers offer introductory discounts. The renewal price may be different, so check both before committing.

6. Can you create a website for free?

Yes, depending on what you want to build.

There are tools that allow you to create and publish simple websites without paying for hosting. For example, static websites can be hosted through services such as Cloudflare Pages.

However, free options may have limitations. You may use a platform-provided web address, have fewer features or need to pay for a custom domain.

For a beginner testing an idea or learning web development, a free option can be a sensible starting point.

You do not need to spend heavily before you understand what your business actually needs.

7. How to avoid overpaying for a website

Before accepting a quotation, take a moment to understand exactly what you are buying.

  • Define your needs: Decide what the website must do before requesting prices.
  • Compare quotations: Ask at least two or three providers to quote for the same requirements.
  • Ask what is included: Check whether domain registration, hosting, content and maintenance are part of the package.
  • Check renewal costs: Do not focus only on the first payment.
  • Confirm ownership: The domain, hosting account and website files should remain accessible to your business.
  • Ask about support: Understand who will help when something stops working.
  • Avoid unnecessary features: Do not pay for complicated functions you will not use.
A useful question to ask any developer: "What exactly will I receive when the website is completed, and what will I need to pay for after launch?"

8. Frequently asked questions

How much does a basic website cost in Kenya?

A simple business website may cost approximately KSh 15,000–50,000 when professionally built. The final price depends on the pages, features, content and provider.

How much does a domain name cost in Kenya?

A .co.ke domain may cost around KSh 1,000–2,500 per year, depending on the registrar and renewal terms. Always confirm the current price.

Is hosting included in website development costs?

Sometimes. Some developers include hosting for a limited period, while others charge separately. Ask what happens when the included period ends.

Can I build a website without coding?

Yes. Website builders and content management systems such as WordPress allow beginners to create websites without writing all the code themselves.

Does a small business need an expensive website?

Not necessarily. A small business should start with a website that solves its most important needs. More features can be added as the business grows.

Conclusion: Spend according to what your business needs

Building a website in Kenya does not have to mean spending a huge amount of money.

The right budget depends on the purpose of your website, the features you need and whether you are building it yourself or hiring someone.

Start with the essentials. Understand the recurring expenses. Compare quotations carefully. Most importantly, make sure the website serves a real purpose for your business.

A useful website is not necessarily the most expensive one. It is one that helps your customers find information, understand your offer and take the next step.
